What is Divide & Conquer?

Definition

12 · CONCLUSION
Learn → Click → Visualize → Run → Understand
Divide and Conquer is an important strategy for solving large problems. It divides a large problem into smaller subproblems, solves them, and combines their results. Algorithms such as Binary Search, Merge Sort and Quick Sort demonstrate how this strategy can make problem solving easier and more efficient.
